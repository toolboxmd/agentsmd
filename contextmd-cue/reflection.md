name: Reflection
applies: A mistake turned into learning, on the turn where it lands. The message shows that a specific claim, plan step or action of this agent was wrong and it should act now: the user, a reviewer, parent or peer, or an external failing check that implicates it, points out its mistake or omission; or the message contradicts what this agent visibly claimed, proposed or did, even without saying so, including an instruction aimed at a defect the window shows in this agent's own output, even when phrased as a requirement for later work. In a council this includes peers disputing this analyst's earlier claim. Also: someone asks this agent for a retrospective or lessons, including a review of a bounded history of agent work, on the turn that asks. It does not continue to later turns. Not: a mistake the agent found through its own check; later turns still carrying out the fix for an earlier correction, or about something else (a new correction landing in this message still counts, even if an earlier mistake in the same incident was already pointed out or acknowledged); a replan, new rule, hold or different choice when nothing was wrong; an approval, permission grant or steering note that shows no specific claim, plan step or action of this agent wrong; a suggestion, new fact or question that does not show it was wrong; being asked to restate or explain its own report ("what do you mean", "explain it better", "I cannot read it"), unless the same message also shows a specific claim, plan step or action of this agent wrong; a suspected omission only to evaluate; ordinary review, repair or coordination of another agent's exposed mistake (a worker's, child's or writer's), even though this agent owns the task, unless its own brief, claim or instruction caused it (a requested retrospective over a bounded history of other agents' work still counts); a failing CI run whose cause or owner is unknown.
