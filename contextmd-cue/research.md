name: Research
applies: The request asks a question whose answer is facts beyond what is at hand, and finding them is what it asks for: how a system works (including explaining it), why it was built that way, what happened before, prior or failed approaches, live machine state to answer a question, or outside-world facts such as a price or a delivery cost; or a new answer to a new question from earlier results or records. The question asked decides, not the source read. Applies to any agent (a worker, a reviewer, an analyst, or a child preparing a decision packet) when the visible request asks for such facts, also as one part of a review or implementation brief that asks to establish and report something beyond the change itself: how other code or another task's work behaves or what it offers, how it maps to or depends on this work, or whether existing work already does it. Checking the change itself is not research, even when it reads code or systems outside the diff: whether it is correct and complete, whether its inputs reach it, and whether its callers, its consumers (including tools that read its output) and the stores it reads or writes work with it. In a council or analyst round it applies while a specific factual claim is disputed and the quoted evidence does not settle it. It continues on later turns while facts the request asked for are still unfound, and stops once they are reported or the work moves on. Not: doing the task itself: reading the code, docs, reference implementation or precedent the brief names in order to implement, port or review it, or reading the records, items or live state the requested work acts on (the item to process, the state to audit or clean); a round that only states final positions or settles wording, including a final round that asks for final positions; a point every side already reports as unknown or untested; a standing instruction to request evidence where needed, by itself; a question whose answer is already written in the window; explaining or restating the agent's own change, report or proposal, which are at hand; reviewing a diff, running tests, or checking a claim against the diff, logs and task records already in hand; investigation the brief does not ask for; finding a defect's or failure's cause or owner, including whether the change introduced or inherited it (diagnosis); the task's own setup and proof facts: its proof command, runtime or setup, proof or baseline evidence for its own change or diagnosis, verifying live that its own deploy or restart took effect, the resources its own task used; a routine gate check; checking one's own running job or a thread just started.
