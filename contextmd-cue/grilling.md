id: agentsmd-grilling
name: Grilling
applies: Several open decisions belonging to the decision owner (the user, or an agent acting for them) must be reasoned through by questioning that owner thoroughly, or the owner asks to be grilled to stress-test their own thinking. Includes a child's decision packet: a brief that has the child work out several open decisions with options and bring them to its parent for answers before implementing, whether or not it is called a packet. It continues on later turns that work those decisions while some stay open. Not: a single proposal or decision; a packet arising mid-implementation; the turn after answers closed the decisions; a turn on other work; answering several design questions with recommendations and no question to the owner; asking an analyst or council to critique a plan; verdicts or debate on options a parent sent; listing choices to answer a plain question; a standing instruction to escalate future decisions.

---
