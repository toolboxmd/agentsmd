The goal is dependable calls at that one desk, and the evidence points to the desk's own link, not the router: other devices on the same router stay connected during the drops, and that desk's old cable runs through a tight grommet, where bends and pinching often cause intermittent faults. I recommend swapping in a new Cat6 patch cable (routed loosely through the grommet) and checking the port and NIC as needed before any network redesign, because a new gateway, switches and Grafana would sit upstream of the likely fault and leave the drops in place.

If calls still drop on the new cable, tell me what the computer reports for link speed and whether the link light flickers during a drop. The UniFi setup is still a fine future-proofing project on its own merits, but I'd treat it as separate from fixing this desk.
