This week, keep the accounting export on your computer and automate the monthly regional sum there.

The workbook already has region, date, and a signed net amount. Refunds are negative, so each board figure is the sum of that amount for one region and one calendar month. Build a PivotTable on the export: rows for region, columns for month, values as the sum of net amount. When the next file arrives, drop it in and refresh. That is the pipeline.

Airbyte, Snowflake, dbt, and Power BI pay off when you have several systems, shared history, and metrics more than one person maintains. One local workbook does not need a connector, a warehouse, or a model project. Hold that budget until a second source appears or the file no longer opens comfortably on your machine.
