You want a reliable monthly net-sales-by-region number for the board without hand work. I recommend skipping the stack for now. One local table that fits on your machine doesn't need Airbyte, Snowflake, dbt or Power BI. A Power Query plus PivotTable in that same workbook can be set up today: load the export, add a month column from the date, and sum the signed amount by region and month so refunds net out automatically. Next month you drop in the new export and click Refresh.

The paid stack starts to make sense when something concrete breaks: several sources need joining, the data outgrows Excel, or other people need live access. Is one of those coming, or is "future-proof" mainly about matching peers?
