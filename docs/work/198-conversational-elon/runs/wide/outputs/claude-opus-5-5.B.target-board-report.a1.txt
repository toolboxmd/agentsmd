You want a correct monthly net-sales-by-region number for the board without the manual summing. For one local Excel table that already holds region, date and signed amounts, the smallest fix is a PivotTable in the workbook itself: paste in the new export, click Refresh, and it groups by month and region with refunds netting out automatically. That takes under an hour, costs nothing, and is less fragile than the hand sums.

I'd hold off on Airbyte, Snowflake, dbt and Power BI. None of them fixes a problem you have today. They add licences, a warehouse and pipelines that someone has to keep running, all for one file that fits on your laptop. They start to pay off when you need several source systems combined, data too big for Excel, or many people on live dashboards; if one of those is coming, tell me which, and we'll size the stack to it.
