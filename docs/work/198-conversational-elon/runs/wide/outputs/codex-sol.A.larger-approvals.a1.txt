We can keep the folder for drafting, but an APPROVED column with typed initials and a naming convention won’t satisfy the signed contract. Authors could change the approved files or approval entries, and initials alone don’t reliably establish who approved which version and when.

For this week’s delivery, I’d keep the shared folder and add a controlled release step:

- A reviewer other than the author approves using an individual authenticated account.
- Each release saves the exact approved file, approver identity, and approval time in a separate archive.
- Authors cannot alter that archive, and retention controls preserve each release for seven years. Later edits stay in the drafting folder and require a new approval.

The APPROVED column can remain a convenient status indicator. Before shipping, we need to verify that the approval record and retained files have those protections; the proposed convention alone leaves the contractual requirements unmet.
