You want a reliable monthly board report that is easy to maintain; I recommend an Excel Power Query pipeline this week, since your single export fits comfortably on your computer.
It would load the export, validate regions, dates and amounts, sum signed net sales by month and region including negative refunds, reconcile the totals to the source, and refresh the board report.
Airbyte, Snowflake and dbt would add cost and maintenance without solving a current bottleneck; keep the transformation rules documented and revisit the stack when multiple sources, data volume or shared reporting create a concrete need.
