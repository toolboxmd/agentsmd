You want a reminder two weeks before each notice deadline so you can decide on every renewal in time. For that, I'd skip the agent stack: n8n, a Gmail agent, a vector database, and a Telegram bot would all be maintaining a problem that a calendar already solves.

**What I recommend instead:** when the statement arrives each January, add one event per contract to your phone calendar, dated two weeks before its notice deadline. Name it something like "Decide: home insurance renewal (notice by 15 Mar)". That's about 15 minutes a year. It uses the app you already check daily, and nothing can cancel or pay on its own.

**Why not the stack:**
- **Vector database:** it's for searching lots of unstructured text. You have one short list of fixed dates.
- **Gmail agent:** the statement comes once a year, so there's nothing to monitor in between.
- **n8n plus Telegram:** this adds a second reminder channel, hosting, credentials, and failure points. A silent workflow failure would mean a missed deadline you'd never notice.
