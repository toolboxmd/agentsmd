You want to decide about each renewal two weeks before its notice deadline, using reminders you’ll actually see.

Since the dates stay fixed for the year, the agents, n8n, vector database, and Telegram bot would add cost and maintenance without improving the reminder; enter the dates once each January and keep cancellation and payment under your control.
