Red conditional formatting on its own doesn't meet the owner's rule. It flags the clash after it's saved, so last week's double booking could happen again. Is the spreadsheet Google Sheets or Excel? Google Sheets has the locking this needs built in (Apps Script `LockService`), and Excel needs a different approach.
