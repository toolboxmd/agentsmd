We can automate this week’s report with a small local pipeline. Given one Excel table that fits comfortably on your computer, I’d hold off on Airbyte, Snowflake and dbt until there’s a concrete need for them.

I’d use Excel Power Query to:

- Read the accounting export and validate region, date and signed net amount.
- Group by month and region, keeping negative refunds in the net sales sums.
- Reconcile the regional totals to the source total and flag missing regions or invalid dates.
- Refresh a board-ready summary, with Power BI added if you need interactive views or shared dashboards.

We’d check the first automated month against your hand-built report and keep the source export for traceability. After that, the monthly process becomes replace the export, refresh and review.

To prepare for future growth, we’d document the input format, calculation rules and checks. Those can move into a larger platform when multiple sources, data volume or access requirements justify it. The budget gives you options; you don’t need to spend it all to make this report reliable.
