I’d start by protecting today’s pickups, then structure the Odoo rollout around pickup deadlines.

Today, filter the shared list to orders due today, sort by pickup time, and assign each unmade cake to a named person. Have one person track completion and flag any cake at risk of missing pickup. Everyone can already see the orders; the immediate gap appears to be preparation priority and ownership.

For next week’s implementation partner, I’d propose:

1. **Define the preparation workflow first.** Each order needs a pickup date and time, a preparation deadline, an owner, and a clear status. The counter’s default view should show overdue and next-due work first.
2. **Pilot the complete flow with a small batch.** Test a prepaid order from sales through ingredient availability, manufacturing, ready-for-pickup status, and collection. Include a later-entered order due sooner to verify that entry order doesn’t determine preparation priority.
3. **Add inventory and manufacturing integration gradually.** Validate recipes, ingredient reservations, and production tasks while preserving the payment and stock reconciliation that already works.
4. **Cut over after staff can reliably run the pickup queue.** Reconcile every open order, choose one authoritative system, train staff, and check upcoming pickups daily during launch.

Make the acceptance criterion concrete: every cake due for pickup has an assigned preparation task and becomes ready before its pickup time. Module synchronization should support that outcome; synchronization alone won’t ensure it.
