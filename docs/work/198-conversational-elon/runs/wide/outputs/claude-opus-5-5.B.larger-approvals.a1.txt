You want the customer's approval controls in place for this week's delivery with as little change as possible. I don't recommend shipping the initials column, because it fails three of the contract's requirements:

- **Approver identity:** any of the 12 authors can type a reviewer's initials.
- **Self-approval:** nothing stops an author from approving their own document.
- **Retained version:** authors can still edit an approved file afterwards, and the column records no reliable approval time.

The smallest fix that meets the contract keeps your folder for drafts and adds two things:

1. **A separate "Released" location.** Only the three reviewers can write to it, and authors can only read.
2. **The platform's built-in approval or versioning.** It records the logged-in approver and the timestamp, and a 7-year retention policy goes on the Released location.

Which platform hosts the shared folder (SharePoint/OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, or something else)? The exact setup depends on it, and on the common ones this can likely be configured before the delivery.
