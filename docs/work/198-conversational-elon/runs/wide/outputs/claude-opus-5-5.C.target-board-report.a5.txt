**Requirements and who asked:** You need monthly net sales by region for the board, computed from the accounting export (region, date, signed net amount, refunds as negatives). The wish to "future-proof" comes from what peers are doing, not from any failure in your report.
**Deleted:** Airbyte, Snowflake, dbt and Power BI. You have one local workbook that fits on your machine, so there is nothing to ingest, warehouse, model or host.
**Bottleneck:** Building the monthly regional sums by hand.
**Checked myself:** Nothing observed. This is based only on what you told me about the export and your process.

**Goal:** Get correct monthly regional net sales into the board report without summing by hand.

**Recommendation:** Add a PivotTable to the export workbook. You can set it up today.

1. Click inside the export table and choose Insert > PivotTable.
2. Put region in Rows and date in Columns, then right-click a date and group it by Months and Years.
3. Put net amount in Values as Sum. Refunds are already negative, so the sums come out net.
4. Each month, paste or replace the new export into the same table and click Refresh.

To check it, compare one month against your last hand-built figures before you rely on it.
