You're trying to make it impossible to promise one item to two customers for overlapping dates, without replacing your tools.

Colours alone can't meet the owner's rule, but you can keep the spreadsheet. I recommend making the sheet read-only for agents and having them add bookings through a small booking form. The form takes a lock, checks that item's existing dates, and refuses to save if they overlap. With the lock, if two agents submit together, one booking saves and the other is rejected. If the sheet is in Google Sheets, this is a short Apps Script with `LockService`, and we could realistically ship it this week.
