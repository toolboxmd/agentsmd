You want a reliable nudge two weeks before each notice deadline so you can decide on each renewal yourself. I'd skip the n8n, Gmail agent, vector database and Telegram bot. Your dates arrive once a year in a single statement and stay fixed, so there is nothing for an agent to watch or retrieve, and that stack would add monthly cost and more things that can break.

Instead, when the statement arrives, spend about 15 minutes adding one event per contract to your phone calendar, dated 14 days before its notice deadline, with the contract name, deadline and renewal date in the title. You already check that calendar every day, and nothing is automated beyond the reminder. Add one more recurring yearly event, "Statement arrived? Enter renewal reminders," so the routine repeats itself.
