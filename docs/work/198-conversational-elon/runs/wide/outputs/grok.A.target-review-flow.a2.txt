The model to launch this week is a backup-approver rule on the tracker you already use.

The last six changes were coded in under a day, then each waited three days because you were the only assigned reviewer and you were away. Two other engineers are already qualified and authorized to approve that work.

Put this in place now:

- On every change, assign you and one of those two engineers as approvers.
- When you are out, either of them approves and the change merges the same day. Their approval is enough.
- Expect a review decision the same business day whenever someone authorized is in.
- Keep status in the current tracker. No new tool.
- On the next few changes, measure time from ready-for-review to merge. While you are absent, that wait should fall from three days to the same day.

Squads, chapters, Jira Align, and quarterly planning workshops are a later decision. They leave this wait in place. A four-person team in one time zone can run the backup rule this week.
