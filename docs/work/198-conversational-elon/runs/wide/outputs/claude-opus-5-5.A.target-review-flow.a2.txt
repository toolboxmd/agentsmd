I'd hold off on squads/chapters and Jira Align. Your last six changes each took under a day to code, then waited three days for approval. Every one had you as the only reviewer while you were away. The bottleneck is a single-reviewer dependency, and new tooling or planning workshops won't fix it. With four engineers in one time zone, you can fix it this week at no cost.

## The operating model

**1. Any one of three qualified reviewers can approve**
- Make you and the two qualified engineers a reviewer group, using a team or CODEOWNERS entry in your repo host.
- Require **one** approval from the group, not approval from you specifically.
- Auto-assign reviews round-robin so the load is spread out by default.

**2. Review response target**
- Reviewers respond within **4 working hours** of a PR being ready. A response means approve, request changes, or hand it off.
- Since you're all in one time zone, this works without on-call schedules.

**3. Absence rule**
- When anyone is out, they mark it in the tracker or calendar, and auto-assignment skips them.
- The author doesn't wait for or ping someone who is away.

**4. Escalation for anything you must see**
- If some changes truly need your sign-off (security, architecture), list those paths explicitly in CODEOWNERS.
- Everything else goes through the shared group. Keep that list short.

**5. Measure it**
- Track time from "PR ready" to "approved" in your existing tracker for the next 2–3 weeks.
- Compare against the current three days. If it drops to hours, you have your answer.

## Rollout this week
- **Day 1:** Agree the rule with the team in a 15-minute conversation: who approves, the response target, and the absence rule.
- **Day 1–2:** Configure the reviewer group, required approvals, and auto-assignment.
- **Day 2 onward:** Re-assign any PRs currently waiting on you to the other two reviewers.
- **In 2–3 weeks:** Review the cycle-time numbers.

## When to revisit the bigger ideas
Squads, chapters, and Jira Align address coordination across many teams. That becomes worth funding at roughly 3+ teams or 25+ engineers, or when you can't see dependencies across teams. If you want to spend money now, small things like PR review reminders in chat or a cycle-time dashboard will pay off more than portfolio tooling.
