These call drops are at that one desk. While the call fails, two other devices on the same router stay connected, so the shared router and internet path are still up. The desk cable is several years old and runs through a tight grommet, which is a common way pairs get pinched and the link starts failing under a video call. A UniFi gateway, managed switches, and a Grafana dashboard all sit upstream of that cable, so they will keep seeing the same desk drop until the cable is replaced.

Before any new gear:

1. Bypass the desk run. Connect that computer with a new, short patch cable that does not pass through the grommet, then place a video call and see whether it holds.
2. If the call holds, replace the desk run with a new Cat6 cable and relieve the grommet so the jacket is not crushed. A pinched cable will fail again.
3. During the test, check the computer’s link speed. A damaged cable often falls back from 1 Gbps to 100 Mbps, or the link drops entirely.

If the new cable stays up through a call, that is the fix. Use the approved budget for UniFi later, as its own project for management and future growth, after this desk link is stable.
