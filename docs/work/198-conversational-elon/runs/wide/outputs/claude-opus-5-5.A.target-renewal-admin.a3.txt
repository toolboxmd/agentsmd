Honestly, I'd skip most of that stack. Your problem is simpler than what those AI assistant builds are designed for.

**What you actually need**

- The dates arrive **once a year**, in **one statement**, and **don't change**.
- You need a nudge **two weeks before each notice deadline**.
- You already check your **phone calendar every day**.
- You **don't want anything acting on your behalf**.

That's a calendar reminder. Nothing needs to watch your inbox, store embeddings, or reason about anything between January and December.

**What I'd suggest instead**

Once a year, when the statement arrives:

1. You give me the statement, either the PDF or the pasted text.
2. I generate a single `.ics` file. Each contract gets:
   - an all-day **"Decide: [contract] renewal"** event 14 days before the notice deadline, with the notice date, renewal date, and contract name in the notes
   - optionally, a second event on the notice deadline itself as a backstop
3. You import the file into your phone calendar once. That takes about two minutes.

Cost is zero, there's nothing to maintain, and nothing runs with access to your Gmail or money. If a reminder fires and you want to cancel, you do it yourself, which matches what you want.

**Why the full stack is a poor fit here**

- **Gmail agent:** you'd be granting read access to your whole inbox to parse one email a year. That's a big privacy and security surface for very little gain.
- **Vector database:** that's for searching lots of unstructured text by meaning. You have a short table of dates.
- **n8n + Telegram:** these work, but you'd be adding a second notification channel next to the calendar you already trust. You'd also have a self-hosted service that has to stay up all year to fire a dozen reminders. If it quietly breaks in March, you won't find out until you've missed a deadline.

**If you still want to build it** (as a learning project, say), the smallest sensible version is:
- one n8n workflow on a daily cron
- reading from a simple sheet or JSON with `contract, renewal_date, notice_date`
- sending a Telegram message when `today == notice_date - 14 days`

You'd enter the dates by hand each January. Leave out the Gmail agent and the vector DB.

Want me to set up the `.ics` generator? It could be a small script you rerun each year, or I can produce the file directly from this year's statement. Since it's October, it would also be worth checking whether any notice deadlines are coming up in the next few weeks.
