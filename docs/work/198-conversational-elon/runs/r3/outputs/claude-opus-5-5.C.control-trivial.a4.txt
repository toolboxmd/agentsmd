`git fetch` downloads new commits and branches from the remote and updates your remote-tracking refs (like `origin/main`), but leaves your local branch and working tree untouched. `git pull` is `git fetch` followed by integrating those changes into your current branch, by merge by default or by rebase if you use `--rebase` or have `pull.rebase` set. Use `fetch` when you want to look before you integrate (for example with `git log HEAD..origin/main`), and `pull` when you just want to catch up.
