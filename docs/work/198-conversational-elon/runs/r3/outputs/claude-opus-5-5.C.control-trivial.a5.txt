`git fetch` downloads new commits and branches from the remote and updates your remote-tracking refs (like `origin/main`), but leaves your working branch and files untouched. `git pull` is `git fetch` followed by integrating those changes into your current branch, by merge by default or rebase if configured (`pull.rebase` or `--rebase`). Use `fetch` when you want to look before you integrate (for example `git log HEAD..origin/main`), and `pull` when you just want to catch up.
