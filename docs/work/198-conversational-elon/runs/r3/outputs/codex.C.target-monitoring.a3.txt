**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central log storage and a Docker migration; neither provides the missing alert by itself.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends email only if it reaches the email step, so startup failures stay silent.
**Checked myself:** No independent checks in this exercise. You reported four missed runs, no other alerts, and an available Synology.

You want an alert when the nightly sync fails to complete. I recommend one hosted dead-man’s-switch monitor: keep cron on `edna`, send the monitor a success heartbeat only after the sync completes, and have it email you when that heartbeat misses the expected completion deadline plus a grace period.

That catches startup crashes and an offline Mac without depending on the failed script to report its own failure.
