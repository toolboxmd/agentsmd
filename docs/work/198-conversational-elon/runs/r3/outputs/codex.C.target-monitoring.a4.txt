**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker; neither is needed to detect a missed run.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends its own email, so a startup crash also silences the alert.
**Checked myself:** Nothing inspected; I’m using your account of the four missed runs and your two machines.

The goal is an alert when the nightly sync does not finish successfully. I recommend one independent daily check on the Synology:

- After a successful sync, `nightly-sync` writes a dated success marker to the NAS.
- After the expected completion deadline, the NAS checks for today’s marker and emails you if it is missing.
- Keep the existing summary email.

This also catches missed cron runs and an unavailable `edna`. Before relying on it, deliberately withhold the marker and verify that the alert arrives. The remaining limitation is that the NAS and its email delivery must be working.
