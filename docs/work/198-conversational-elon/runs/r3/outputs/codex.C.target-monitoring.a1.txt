**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker for this fix. Neither makes a missing email trigger an alert.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends its own notification only after reaching the email step, so startup failures stay silent.
**Checked myself:** No live checks; your account establishes four missed runs and no independent alert.

You’re trying to catch a missing successful run. I recommend one small daily check on the Synology, using its built-in scheduler.

Have `nightly-sync` record a completion timestamp on the NAS only after a successful sync. After the normal finishing window, the NAS checks that timestamp and emails you if today’s completion is missing. A missing or unreadable timestamp must count as an alert, and the NAS’s email credentials must be independent of the sync token.

That catches startup crashes, hangs, cron failing to start, and `edna` being offline. Keep the existing summary email, then deliberately skip one run to prove the NAS alert arrives.
