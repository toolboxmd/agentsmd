For this case, don't build that stack — it adds cost, maintenance, and failure modes for no benefit.

Your problem is: once a year, copy fixed dates, then get reminded once per contract 2 weeks before its notice deadline. You already check your phone calendar daily, and you don't want auto-actions.

Do this instead:

1. In January when the statement arrives, for each contract create one calendar event on `notice deadline - 14 days`: e.g. `Decide: Home Insurance - notice by Mar 15, renews Apr 15`
2. Put the details in the event notes and set the phone alert you normally use.
3. Keep the statement as backup.

You don't need:
- Gmail agent: it's one document to transcribe per year, manual is faster and more reliable
- Vector DB: it's a small structured list, not semantic search
- n8n / Telegram bot: it would just duplicate your calendar in a place you check less often

Save the budget — this is a 15-minute calendar task once a year.
