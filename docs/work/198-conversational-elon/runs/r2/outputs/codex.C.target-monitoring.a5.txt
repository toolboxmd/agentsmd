**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker are unnecessary for alerting on this one job.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends email only if execution reaches the email step.
**Checked myself:** From your account, an expired token caused four startup crashes with no notification; I have not inspected either machine.

I recommend an external dead-man’s-switch monitor: `nightly-sync` sends it a success signal only after completing, and the monitor emails you if that signal misses its daily deadline. Set the deadline after 03:00 with enough allowance for the job’s normal runtime; this also catches cron failing to start it or `edna` being offline.

Fix the expired token, then deliberately skip a success signal to verify that the alert reaches you. The Synology can stay out of this setup.
