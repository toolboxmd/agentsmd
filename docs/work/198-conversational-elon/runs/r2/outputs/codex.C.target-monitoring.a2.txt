**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker are unnecessary for that outcome.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends its own email, so a startup crash also silences the alert.
**Checked myself:** From what you told me, cron runs it at 03:00 on `edna`, four startup crashes went unnoticed, and no independent alert exists.

I recommend one external dead-man’s-switch monitor: give it the expected daily completion deadline, have `nightly-sync` ping it only after a successful run, and let the monitor email you when that ping is missing. That covers startup crashes, cron failing to launch it, and `edna` being offline; hosting the monitor outside your home also covers a home power or internet outage.

Set the deadline using the job’s normal runtime plus a margin, then deliberately skip a ping to verify the alert reaches you. The Synology can stay out of this setup.
