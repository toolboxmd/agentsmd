You want to hear about it when the sync doesn't succeed, without reading anything. A central log won't do that: you'd still have to look at it, and a job that never starts writes nothing.

I recommend a dead man's switch instead. The job pings a URL when it succeeds, and if no ping arrives by about 04:00 you get an email or push alert. That catches crashes, expired tokens, cron not firing, and `edna` being off or asleep.

Use hosted healthchecks.io (free tier) rather than self-hosting it on the Synology. That way the alert still fires if the NAS or your home network is down too.

The change is one HTTP call after the summary email in `nightly-sync`, plus a check set to "daily at 03:00, 1h grace." No Docker needed.
