The goal is to get alerted when the nightly sync doesn't succeed. I recommend a dead man's switch: a free healthchecks.io check that expects a ping every 24 hours, plus one line at the end of `nightly-sync` that sends that ping after a successful run. If the ping doesn't arrive, because of a crash, an expired token, `edna` being off, or cron not running, healthchecks.io emails you. Since it runs off-site, it also works when your home network is down, which a monitor on the Synology can't do.
