**Goal:** find out when `nightly-sync` doesn't succeed, without reading anything.

A central log won't do that, because it only stores failures you'd still have to go read. I'd skip the log and Docker and add a dead man's switch instead:

- The script pings a check URL, such as a free Healthchecks.io check, only after it succeeds.
- If no ping arrives by about 04:00, the service emails or notifies you.
- This catches the expired-token crash, cron not firing, and `edna` being off or asleep.

I'd host the check outside your house rather than on the Synology. A monitor at home goes quiet too if the power or internet there drops.

If you're okay creating a Healthchecks.io account, send me the ping URL and I'll add the one-line ping to the script.
