**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker from this fix; neither supplies the missing alert by itself.

**Checked myself:** Your account establishes four missed runs and no independent alerting; I haven’t inspected the setup.

The goal is to know when the nightly sync hasn’t completed successfully. I recommend one external heartbeat monitor:

- Keep cron and the Python script on `edna`.
- Send the monitor a success ping only after the sync completes successfully.
- Have the monitor expect that ping after each 03:00 run, with a grace period for normal runtime.
- If it’s missing, the monitor emails you independently of `edna`.
- Test it by deliberately withholding a ping and confirming the alert arrives.

An external monitor also catches `edna` being off or your home connection failing. The Synology doesn’t need to be involved.
