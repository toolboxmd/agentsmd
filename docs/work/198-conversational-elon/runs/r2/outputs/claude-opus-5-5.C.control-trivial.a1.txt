`git fetch` downloads new commits and branches from the remote and updates your remote-tracking refs (like `origin/main`), but leaves your local branches and working tree untouched. `git pull` does a fetch and then immediately integrates the result into your current branch, by merge by default or by rebase with `--rebase` or `pull.rebase=true`. Use `fetch` when you want to inspect incoming changes (for example `git log HEAD..origin/main`) before deciding how to integrate them.
