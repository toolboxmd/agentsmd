`git fetch` downloads new commits and branches from the remote and updates your remote-tracking refs (like `origin/main`), but leaves your working tree and local branches untouched. `git pull` is `git fetch` followed by merging (or rebasing, with `--rebase` or `pull.rebase=true`) the upstream branch into your current branch. Use `fetch` when you want to inspect incoming changes first (for example `git log HEAD..origin/main`), and `pull` when you just want to update.
