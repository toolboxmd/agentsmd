What you're trying to achieve: know within minutes when `nightly-sync` fails, without reading logs yourself. A central log server doesn't solve that; it still requires you to look. The actual bottleneck is "job failed, nobody told me," and that's a monitoring/alerting problem, not a logging problem.

Recommendation: add a dead-man's-switch check-in (e.g. Healthchecks.io self-hosted on the Synology, or a tiny cron+script that pings a watchdog), where `nightly-sync` pings "start" and "success" each run, and you get a push/email alert if the ping doesn't arrive by 03:15. That would have caught the expired-token crash on night one. Central logging on the Synology is a reasonable *separate* upgrade later if you want searchable history, but it's not what prevents a repeat of last week, and "everything in Docker" is scope creep for this problem.
