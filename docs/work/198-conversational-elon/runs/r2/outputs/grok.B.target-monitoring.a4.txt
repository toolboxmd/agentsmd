You want to know the morning nightly-sync dies, including when it crashes before its own email. A central log on the Synology stays empty in that case, and you will not read it anyway.

Use hosted Healthchecks.io: the script sends one ping only after a successful run, and the check emails you if that ping does not show up within a grace period after 03:00, from a monitor that still works when the Mac mini or the house network is down.
