`git fetch` downloads new commits and branches from the remote into your remote-tracking refs (like `origin/main`) but leaves your working branch and files untouched. `git pull` does a fetch and then immediately merges (or rebases, if configured with `pull.rebase`) the upstream branch into your current branch. Use `fetch` when you want to inspect incoming changes first (`git log HEAD..origin/main`), and `pull` when you just want to update.
