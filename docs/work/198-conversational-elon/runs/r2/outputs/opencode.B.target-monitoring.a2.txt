What you are trying to achieve is to know by morning if nightly-sync on edna failed or never ran, without checking logs.
A central log on the Synology would not have caught the four day outage because nothing watches it, and moving everything to Docker adds failure modes without fixing silent failure.
I recommend a dead man's switch instead: edna sends a success signal on completion and something outside your home alerts you if it is missing or failed, should I outline that minimal change?
