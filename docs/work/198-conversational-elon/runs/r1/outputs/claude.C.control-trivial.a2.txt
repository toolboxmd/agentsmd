`git fetch` downloads commits/branches/tags from the remote into your local repo's remote-tracking branches (e.g. `origin/main`), but doesn't touch your working branch or files. `git pull` does a fetch and then immediately merges (or rebases, with `--rebase`) the fetched changes into your current branch. Use `fetch` when you want to see what's changed before deciding what to do with it; `pull` when you just want to update now.
