`git fetch` downloads commits/branches/tags from the remote but doesn't touch your working directory or local branches, it just updates your remote-tracking branches (e.g. `origin/main`). `git pull` does a `fetch` and then immediately merges (or rebases, with `--rebase`) the fetched changes into your current branch. Fetch is the "look before you leap" option; pull is fetch + merge in one step.
