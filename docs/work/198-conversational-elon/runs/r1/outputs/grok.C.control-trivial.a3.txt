`git fetch` downloads new commits, branches, and tags from the remote and updates your remote-tracking refs (such as `origin/main`), leaving your current branch and working tree unchanged. `git pull` does that fetch and then integrates the remote branch into your current branch, by merge by default, or by rebase when `pull.rebase` is set. Fetch when you want to inspect what arrived first; pull when you want those commits on your branch immediately.
