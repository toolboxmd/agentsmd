The actual gap isn't missing logs, it's that nothing alerted on the *absence* of success — log aggregation wouldn't have caught this either, since the job never got far enough to log anything useful. I'd add a dead-man's-switch first: self-host [Healthchecks.io](https://github.com/healthchecks/healthchecks) (or use ntfy) in a Docker container on the Synology, have `nightly-sync` ping it at start and end, and get pushed/emailed if the ping doesn't show up by, say, 03:15 — that alone would've caught the token expiry on night one instead of night four. Centralized logs are a nice-to-have for post-mortems (Dozzle is genuinely Pieter-Levels-simple: one container, zero config, browser view of all your Docker logs) but layer that on *after* the heartbeat, not instead of it, since you said you won't go looking at logs anyway.
