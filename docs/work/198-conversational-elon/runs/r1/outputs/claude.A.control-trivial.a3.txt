`git fetch` downloads commits/branches/tags from the remote into your local repo's remote-tracking branches (e.g. `origin/main`), but doesn't touch your working branch or files. `git pull` does a fetch and then immediately merges (or rebases, if configured) the fetched changes into your current branch. So `fetch` is the "safe, look-before-you-leap" option, while `pull` = fetch + merge in one step.
