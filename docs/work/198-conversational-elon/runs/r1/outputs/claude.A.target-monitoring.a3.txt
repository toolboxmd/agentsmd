Centralized logging won't actually catch this failure mode — the crash happened before any log/email was even written, so a log aggregator only helps you investigate after you've *already* noticed something's wrong, and you said you won't be reading logs anyway. I'd prioritize a dead man's switch instead: have `nightly-sync` ping a heartbeat endpoint at start (and end), and alert you if the ping doesn't arrive by ~03:10 — self-hosted via Uptime Kuma in Docker on the Synology (push monitors) is a good fit and gets you a real-time push/email alert on silence, not just a nicer log viewer.

If you still want the Pieter Levels-style central log for other containers/services down the road, that's a reasonable separate project (Loki+Grafana or just Dozzle), but I'd treat it as a bonus, not the fix for this specific incident. Want me to spec out the Uptime Kuma heartbeat setup first?
