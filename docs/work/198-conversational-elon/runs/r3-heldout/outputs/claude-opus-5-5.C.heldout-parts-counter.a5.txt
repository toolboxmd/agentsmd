Setup, assuming codes are in column A, shelf in B, and price in C on a sheet called `Parts`:

1. Add a new sheet called `Lookup` and drag it to the first tab position so the workbook opens on it.
2. In `A1` type `Code`, `A2` type `Shelf`, `A3` type `Price`.
3. In `B2` enter `=XLOOKUP(B1,Parts!A:A,Parts!B:B,"Not found")`
4. In `B3` enter `=XLOOKUP(B1,Parts!A:A,Parts!C:C,"Not found")` and format it as currency.
5. Make `B1` large and bold, then save.

At the counter, click `B1`, type the code, press Enter, and the shelf and price appear. Edits you make on `Parts` show up immediately.
