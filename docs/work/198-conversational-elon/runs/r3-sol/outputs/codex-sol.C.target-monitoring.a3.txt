**Requirements and who asked:** You want to hear when `nightly-sync` fails, without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and moving everything into Docker for this fix.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends email only if it reaches that step; nothing independently notices a failed or missing run.
**Checked myself:** From your report: cron runs it at 03:00 on `edna`, and four startup failures produced no notification.

You want reliable failure alerts with minimal upkeep; I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor, Healthchecks.io. Have a small cron wrapper report success or failure independently of the Python script, and configure the monitor to email you if the expected completion signal never arrives, allowing for normal runtime and a grace period. That covers startup crashes and missed runs, including when `edna` is offline, while keeping your existing summary email.
