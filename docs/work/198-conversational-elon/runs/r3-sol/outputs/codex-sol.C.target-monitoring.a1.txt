**Checked myself:** Only your supplied facts; I haven’t inspected the machines or verified a service.

To catch missed successful runs, I recommend one hosted heartbeat check, such as Healthchecks.io, with email alerts. Have `nightly-sync` ping it only after success, with a daily deadline of 03:00 plus its normal runtime and a grace period.

A missing ping then triggers an alert even if the script crashes at startup, cron never starts it, or `edna` is offline.
