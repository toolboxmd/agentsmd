You want an alert when `nightly-sync` fails or never runs, without reading logs.
I recommend an external heartbeat monitor: a cron wrapper on `edna` reports success only after Python exits successfully, reports failures immediately, and the monitor alerts you if completion is overdue after the daily 03:00 run.
Keep cron for now; central logs on the Synology can help diagnose failures later, but the missing piece is an alert that still works when `edna` cannot send anything.
