Requirements and who asked: Alert within a day on fail or silence with no log reading (user need for nightly-sync); Synology Docker central log was user suggestion only.
Deleted: Central log on Synology, Docker logging stack, log aggregation for manual review.
Bottleneck: No detection of missing success, crashes before email left four nights silent.
Checked myself: Single cron job on edna at 03:00, email only on finish, four startup crashes on expired token with no email, no other alerting today, Synology available, you will not read logs.

What you are trying to achieve is know by morning if nightly-sync missed or failed without reading logs.
I recommend one dead-man heartbeat that nightly-sync pings on success and that alerts you if no ping arrives, because it catches the exact failure you had.
Do not build a central log on Synology in Docker, it adds a system to maintain and still requires you to look, so it would have stayed silent for those four days too.
