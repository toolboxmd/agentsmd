**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker for this task; neither is needed to detect a missed sync.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends its own email, so a startup crash also silences notification.
**Checked myself:** Only your account of the setup and failure; no independent checks.

Your goal is reliable failure notification. I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor: cron sends it a success signal only after `nightly-sync` completes successfully, and the monitor emails you if that signal misses the daily deadline.

Set that deadline to 03:00 Europe/Warsaw plus the job’s normal runtime and a reasonable buffer. This catches startup crashes, skipped cron runs, and `edna` being offline without adding anything to maintain on the Synology. Verify it by deliberately withholding one heartbeat and confirming the alert arrives.
