**Requirements and who asked:** You want to hear when `nightly-sync` fails, without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker; neither is needed to detect a missing nightly run.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends its own notification, so an early crash also kills the alert.
**Checked myself:** Only your account: four startup failures, no emails, and no independent monitoring.

Your goal is reliable failure alerts with minimal upkeep; I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor that emails you when the nightly success signal is missing. Have `nightly-sync` ping it only after a successful sync, and configure a daily deadline after 03:00 with enough allowance for normal runtime. That catches startup crashes, missed cron runs, and `edna` being offline, without making the Synology another monitoring system you maintain.
