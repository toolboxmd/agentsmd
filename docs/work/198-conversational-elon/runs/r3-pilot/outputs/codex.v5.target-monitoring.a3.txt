**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker; neither is needed to detect a missing nightly run.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends its own email, so a startup crash also silences the notification.
**Checked myself:** Only your supplied facts: cron runs at 03:00 on `edna`, four startup failures produced no email, and no independent alert exists.

Your goal is an automatic alert when the nightly sync does not finish successfully. I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor: have the Python script send it a success signal after completing the sync, and configure it to alert you if that signal has not arrived by the normal completion time plus a grace period.

Keep cron and the existing summary email. This catches startup crashes, missed cron runs, and `edna` being offline because the alert comes from outside the Mac; the Synology needs no new containers or maintenance.
