**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker infrastructure for this one job.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends email only after reaching its summary step, so a startup crash stays silent.
**Checked myself:** Only the facts you supplied; I have not inspected the job or machines.

I recommend one external dead-man’s-switch monitor: `nightly-sync` sends it a success heartbeat after completing, and the monitor emails you if that heartbeat misses its daily deadline. Set the deadline after 03:00 with enough allowance for its normal runtime.

That covers startup crashes, cron failing to launch it, and `edna` being offline. A central log on the Synology would collect evidence, but you would still need something to notice missing runs and alert you; hosting the monitor at home also leaves it vulnerable to the same home power or internet outage. Keep the existing summary email and cron job; the missing piece is an independent alert when successful completion never arrives.
