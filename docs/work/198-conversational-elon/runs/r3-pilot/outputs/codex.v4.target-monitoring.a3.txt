**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging, moving the job into Docker, and running monitoring on the Synology.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends email only if it reaches that step; nothing independently notices silence.
**Checked myself:** Only your account: cron starts it at 03:00 on `edna`, and startup failures went unnoticed for four nights.

The goal is an alert when the nightly sync does not finish successfully. I recommend one hosted heartbeat check in Healthchecks.io: send it a ping only after a successful sync, and have it email you if that ping is missing after 03:00 plus a grace period based on the job’s normal runtime. That catches startup crashes, missed cron runs, and `edna` being offline; central logs would still need something watching for the missing run.
