**Deleted:** a central Synology/Docker log-aggregation stack (ELK/Grafana/Loki style). It doesn't fix the actual problem, you already said you won't read logs, and there's nothing else on edna today that needs cross-job log search.
**Bottleneck:** there's no alert on a missing expected event. The job can die before it ever reaches the email step, so a crash and a quiet success day look identical to you.
**Checked myself:** this is from what you've told me, cron on edna at 03:00, success sends an email, it crashed at the token step four nights running with zero other alerting in place.

You're trying to get notified the moment the job fails to complete, not to search logs across services. The smallest thing that does that is a dead man's switch: a hosted free heartbeat service (e.g. healthchecks.io) that the script pings once at start and once on successful completion, with a grace window past 03:00. If the ping doesn't arrive, it alerts you by email/push, no container, nothing new to run or watch on the Synology at all. I'd skip a self-hosted heartbeat container on the NAS too, since it's one more thing you'd have to keep alive for no added benefit over the hosted version. Concretely: add two curl lines to nightly_sync.py (one at the top, one right before the existing summary email), create one free check with a ~90-minute grace period, done.
