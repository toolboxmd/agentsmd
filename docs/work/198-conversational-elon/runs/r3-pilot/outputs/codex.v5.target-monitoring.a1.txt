**Requirements and who asked:** You want to hear when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker; neither is needed to detect a missed run.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends its own notification, so a startup crash silences it.
**Checked myself:** Only your account: four startup failures, no email, and no independent alert.

The goal is an alert whenever the nightly sync fails to finish. I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor outside `edna`: the script pings it only after a successful sync, and the monitor emails you if that daily ping is overdue.

Set the deadline to 03:00 plus the job’s normal maximum runtime and a little margin. This catches startup crashes, hangs, missed cron runs, and `edna` being offline; keep the existing summary email and leave the Synology out of this.
