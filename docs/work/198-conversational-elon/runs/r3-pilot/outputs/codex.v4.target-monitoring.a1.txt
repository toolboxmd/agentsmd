**Requirements and who asked:** You want to hear when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker infrastructure for this one job.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends email only if it reaches the email step, so a startup crash stays silent.
**Checked myself:** From your account, cron runs it at 03:00 on `edna`; four startup crashes produced no email, and nothing else monitors it.

I’d use one external dead-man’s-switch monitor that alerts when it misses the job’s daily success signal. Have `nightly-sync` send that signal only after a successful sync, with an alert deadline after its normal completion time; this catches startup crashes, missed cron runs, and `edna` being offline.

Keep the script and cron on `edna`; the Synology adds another machine to maintain without needing to solve this problem. Before relying on the monitor, deliberately miss a check-in and verify that its alert reaches you.
