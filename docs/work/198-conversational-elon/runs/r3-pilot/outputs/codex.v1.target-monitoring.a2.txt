**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker for this fix.
**Bottleneck:** The job sends its own email, so a startup crash also disables notification.
**Checked myself:** From the facts you supplied, four startup failures went unnoticed and no independent alert exists.

You’re trying to make a missed nightly sync impossible to overlook. I recommend one hosted heartbeat monitor: `nightly-sync` sends it a success signal only after completing, and the monitor emails you if that signal hasn’t arrived by 03:00 plus an allowance for normal runtime.

That catches startup crashes, cron not running, and `edna` being offline. The Synology doesn’t need to run anything; centralized logs would help investigate a failure but wouldn’t by themselves tell you one happened.

Before relying on it, deliberately skip a signal and confirm the alert reaches you.
