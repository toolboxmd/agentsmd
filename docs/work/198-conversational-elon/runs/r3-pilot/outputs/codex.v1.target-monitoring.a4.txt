**Requirements and who asked:** You want an alert when `nightly-sync` fails, without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker infrastructure for this one job.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends its own email, so an early crash also kills the notification.
**Checked myself:** Only what you told me: four missed runs, no independent alert, and one daily cron job on `edna`.

The goal is to make a missed sync visible automatically. I recommend one externally hosted heartbeat monitor: after a successful sync, `edna` sends it a small “completed” signal; if that signal misses the expected completion deadline plus a buffer, the monitor emails you.

This catches startup crashes, hangs, and `edna` being offline. Hosting the monitor outside your home also lets it detect a home power or internet outage.

Set the deadline using the job’s usual runtime, then deliberately skip a signal and verify that the alert arrives before trusting it.
