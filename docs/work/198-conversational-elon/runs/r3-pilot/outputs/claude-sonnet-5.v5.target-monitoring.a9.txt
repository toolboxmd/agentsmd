Requirements and who asked: you need to know within the hour when nightly-sync fails, without reading logs. Deleted: a central Synology log stack (Docker, Loki/ELK-style aggregation) — that's for searching historical logs across many services, not for "did the one job I have run tonight," and it adds a server you'd have to maintain and watch itself. Bottleneck: the job dies before it ever reaches the email step, so the fix is a check that fires independent of the script reaching that point, not where logs live. Checked myself: you told me it emails on success and crashed silently four nights before anyone noticed, and nightly-sync is your only scheduled job.

Smallest fix: wrap the cron call in a dead-man's-switch ping (e.g. a free Healthchecks.io-style check, or self-hosted later if you ever have more than one job) — cron curls it on start and on success, and if the ping doesn't land by 03:10 you get an alert on your phone. No Synology, no Docker, no new always-on service to watch.
