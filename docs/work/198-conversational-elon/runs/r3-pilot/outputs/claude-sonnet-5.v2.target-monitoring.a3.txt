What you're trying to achieve: know within minutes when `nightly-sync` fails to run, without reading logs.

A central Synology log won't fix this: the job crashed before it could write or email anything, so a log aggregator just gives you a prettier place to see nothing arrived. The actual bottleneck is "no one checks that the job ran" — that needs a dead-man's-switch, not more logging infrastructure.

Recommendation: add a single `curl` heartbeat ping at the end of `nightly-sync` to a dead-man's-switch service (e.g. Healthchecks.io, free tier), configured to alert you by email/push if it doesn't check in by 03:15. That's one new thing to maintain, catches exactly the failure you had (crash before completion), and needs no Docker, no NAS container, no log pipeline.
