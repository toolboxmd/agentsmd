**Requirements and who asked:** You want to know when `nightly-sync` fails without reading logs.
**Deleted:** Central logging and Docker infrastructure; neither is needed to detect a missed run.
**Bottleneck:** The script sends email only after finishing, so a startup crash leaves you without an alert.
**Checked myself:** Only the facts you supplied: cron runs it at 03:00 on `edna`, and an expired token caused four silent failures.

You want silent failures to become visible: I recommend one external dead-man’s-switch monitor that emails you when a nightly success signal is missing. Have the script send that signal only after a successful sync, and set the deadline after its normal completion time with a little margin; this also catches `edna` being offline or cron never starting it.

A central log on the Synology would collect information you already said you will not read. Leave the job on `edna`; the remaining decision is how late a successful run can reasonably finish before you should get an alert.
