# Objective

Produce decision-ready evidence on whether a risk-based Sol, Terra, Luna, and
Spark hierarchy reduces accepted completion time and token cost without
lowering quality on representative ToolboxMD development work.

The Objective is complete when:

- Answer-hidden, offline-verifiable fixtures cover a low-complexity multi-host
  contract task, a medium cross-file behavior task, and a hard asynchronous
  state-machine task reconstructed from exact accepted ToolboxMD history.
- A no-model preflight proves local candidate isolation, fixture
  discrimination, route identity, timeout behavior, verifier separation, and
  telemetry attribution before quota is spent.
- Every decision-relevant unique model and effort comparison runs once under
  one inclusive 600-second implementation-and-review lifecycle, without retry
  or automatic repair.
- Every safely completed implementation that passes exact allowed-path and
  telemetry-attribution gates receives deterministic public and hidden
  verification and read-only Luna max review. Unsafe scope or telemetry stops
  the cell before either stage.
- Results attribute end-to-end and per-stage wall time, time to first verified
  artifact, input, cached input, uncached input, output, reasoning output,
  quota movement, verifier outcomes, review findings, and repair load without
  double counting.
- One shuffled anonymous Grok xhigh comparison evaluates the locked accepted
  artifacts.
- The final report recommends adopt, narrow, replicate, or reject and
  identifies the lowest-load reliable routing surface supported by the
  evidence.

Changing the global routing contract, modifying the source product
repositories, claiming a universal model ranking, and retrying failed routes
are outside this Objective.
